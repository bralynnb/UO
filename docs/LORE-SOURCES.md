- **Interstellar Market in New York**: June 19, 1984 signal, resource/token economy, required market employment for residency, probationary human portal access, controlled market boundaries.

The original source documents remain separate from this repository.

The current request explicitly places the game in 1985. The older explanatory lore document describes a period of a few years after the 1984 signal before selection. This build follows the requested 1985 date without rewriting the source document or claiming a resolved canon chronology.

Confirmed source concepts: Earth money is not market currency; exchange centers standardize resource value into tokens; off-world portal access is restricted for humans; bags carry and protect belongings; market residents work at market-related jobs. No weapon purchases or magical spell system are included.

Game adaptations: this fictional block layout, dimensions, street closure, exact dialogue, seed exchange rate, delivery route/rewards, shop prices, litter recycling reputation, and per-guest pickups. These are prototype mechanics, not new asserted canon. City props are period-inspired generic models rather than historically authenticated reconstructions of a specific intersection or car model.

No source documents were changed. No unrelated Drive content is bundled.
