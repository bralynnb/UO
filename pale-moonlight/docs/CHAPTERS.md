# Ten-chapter structure

Only Chapter 1 is implemented. Later chapters are broad planning notes, not playable rooms or episode transcripts.

## 1. The Quiet Border — playable

Review the losses, prepare a strategic assessment, and ask Garak to investigate.

## 2. Old Acquaintances — planned

The search for reliable intelligence tests the limits of conventional methods.

## 3. An Unwelcome Proposal — planned

Sisko weighs a different route to Romulan involvement.

## 4. The Specialist — planned

An unreliable specialist becomes part of the undertaking.

## 5. The Price of Cooperation — planned

Private arrangements create public consequences on the station.

## 6. A Convincing Record — planned

The operation requires something that can withstand scrutiny.

## 7. Necessary Supplies — planned

Sisko confronts the cost of obtaining what the operation needs.

## 8. The Visitor — planned

A Romulan visitor puts the undertaking to its decisive test.

## 9. The Consequence — planned

Events move beyond Sisko's direct control.

## 10. The Private Record — planned

The political outcome leaves Sisko to confront his own responsibility.
