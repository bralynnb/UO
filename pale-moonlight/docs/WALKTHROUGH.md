# Chapter 1 walkthrough

1. In Sisko's office, **Use** the secure terminal on the desk to review the casualty report.
2. **Pick up** the command PADD on the front-left of the desk.
3. Click the right-hand door to enter Ops.
4. **Use** the central strategic display to review the Romulan border.
5. Choose **Use**, click the command PADD in the inventory, then click the strategic display to record the assessment.
6. Click the right-hand turbolift to go to Garak's shop.
7. Choose **Talk to**, then click Garak.
8. Choose the question about his old contacts obtaining Dominion intelligence.
9. Finish the conversation. Chapter 1 completes and saves.

You may inspect other objects, visit locations early, and leave conversations without losing progress. The critical conversation choice appears only after the assessment is complete. F1 gives the next relevant hint. The story continues in the planned Chapter 2; it is not playable in this build.
